Game Design Document

ZOMBYDASH

Top-down action / survival — a cartoony, no-combat take on the zombie chase, built in Unreal Engine.

Unreal Engine PC / Mobile Ages 10+ Survival · No Combat
01 · Overview

Genre

Top-down Action / Survival

Platform

PC / Mobile

Art Style

Cartoony, colorful, exaggerated animations and characters — bold outlines, expressive characters, and humorous zombie designs.

Target Audience

Ages 10+ — casual players who enjoy light survival challenges and progression-based gameplay.

02 · Core Gameplay

Objective

Escape from waves of cartoon-style zombies and reach the Doctor to heal. Accumulate 200 health points to progress to the next level.

Game Loop

  • Player starts with low health (e.g., 50 HP)
  • Zombies chase the player continuously
  • The player navigates the map to avoid zombies and reach healing stations (the Doctor)
  • Each visit to the Doctor increases HP
  • On reaching 200 HP, the player auto-advances to the next level
03 · Controls

PC

  • WASD / Arrow Keys — move player
  • Secret Keys — figure them out throughout the game!
04 · Characters

Player

  • Cartoony human character
  • Starts with minimal health
  • Fast, agile
  • Limited stamina for dashing

Zombies

  • Silly, exaggerated zombies with different looks
  • Random speeds and movements
  • Increase in numbers as the game progresses

Doctor

  • Friendly NPC
  • Heals the player on touch (+20 HP)

06 · Level Design

Layout

  • Top-down, semi-open maps
  • Obstacles, walls, and environmental hazards
  • Randomly spawning zombies
  • Doctor appears at a safe spot each time after use

Progression

  • More zombies each level
  • Faster zombies
  • Traps or distractions (e.g., fake doctors, banana peels)
  • Optional upgrades (speed boosts, decoys, etc.)
07 · Art & Sound

Art Style

  • Bright, vibrant palette
  • Exaggerated, bouncy animations
  • Fun UI with comic-style HP bar and effects

Sound

  • Funky, upbeat background music
  • Comical zombie groans
  • Game over sound
  • Sound cue on triggering a new level
